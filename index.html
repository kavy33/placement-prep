Project explanation framework

Prepare a clear 90-second walkthrough.

01Context — who had the problem, and why did it matter?
02Your contribution — what did you personally design and implement?
03Engineering choices — what alternatives did you consider?
04Evidence — what changed? Include metrics, tests, or user feedback.
05Reflection — what would you improve with another week?
